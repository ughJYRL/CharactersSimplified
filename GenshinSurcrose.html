Sucrose

Anemo/Catalyst/4*

Kit Overview
obtainable through gacha and the monthly store. Sucrose is currently one of the best Anemo units as she can support a variety of team because of her kit her skill has a medium-sized AoE (and with a con, get a second charge, allowing for faster rotations). she also shares EM to the whole team

Role

- she works best as a support, offering Res Shred as well as extra EM
- her skill and burst are easy enough to use, you only need to Swirl the element you need to get Res Shred on

Build

- as a support, sucrose only needs to build ER to get her Burst consistently
- she only wants weapons that give ER or EM, and her best set is Viridescent Verener for Res Shred
