Kaeya

Cryo/Sword/4*

Kit Overview
Kaeya is a unit given to the player for free at the start of the game, he is a Character that can either be a Main or Sub-DPS. his Skill deal cryo dmg in front of him, and his burst creates rotating pieces of ice around the active unit that deal and apply cryo when an enemy makes contact

Role

- he works best as a Sub-DPS with his Burst
- his skill does not have that high a scaling, but it is on a short cooldown, allowing for faster rotations as a Main DPS

Build

- as a Sub-DPS, focus on building ER + EM for quicker energy for more Bursts
- he works well as an off-fielder for Melt but also as a reverse melt Main DPS
