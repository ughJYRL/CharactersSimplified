Amber

Pyer/Bow/4*

Kit Overview

    Amber is a unit given to the player for free
    at the start of the game, She is a Character
    focused on being a "Main DPS". her Skill deploys
    a "Baron Bunny" that has a taunt, although its
    not that strong while her Burst creates a small
    AoE of Pyro for a few short seconds

DPS Role (preferred Role)

    - she works best as a DPS, although a weak one
    - focus on building her Crit stats (rate & DMG)
    - Melt teams are better for her due to her slow
    Pyro Application, allowing her to do forward
    Melts (Cryo + Pyro)

Sustain

    - her only form of "sustain" is Baron Bunny's
    taunt, but its not strong enough even for small mobs
    and bosses ignore the taunt fully

Support

    - theoretically, she works as an off-field
    Pyro Applicator, but Baron Bunny takes too long to
    explode and you need to funnel Energy for her Burst
