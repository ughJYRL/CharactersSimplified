Amber

Pyer/Bow/4*

Kit Overview
Amber is a unit given to the player for free at the start of the game, She is a Character focused on being a "Main DPS". her Skill deploys a "Baron Bunny" that has a taunt, although its not that strong. her Burst creates a small AoE of Pyro for a few short seconds

Role

- she works best as a DPS, although a weak one
- her main DPS (especially at higher con level) comes mainly from her charge attacks, where she gets extra damage from hitting weak points

Build

- focus on building up her Crit stats
- for melt teams, get her a bit of EM otherwise, focus on ATK% with a bit of Pyro DMG bonus
